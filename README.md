Ember Lantern Duel — an original card duel against the Moth Queen. Two lanterns, 30 life each. Ember refills every turn and grows by one a turn to 10. Play creatures from your hand, cast spells, and let your row attack at the end of your turn: each creature hits the creature across from it, or the enemy lantern when the row across is empty. Break her lantern first to win; lose yours and the duel is lost. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best card game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or open `game/index.html` directly in desktop Chrome. No network requests, no build step.

## How to play

- Click a card in your hand to pick it, then click your row to summon a creature, or click an enemy creature / her life bar to aim Ember Burst. Click a spell card twice to cast it when it needs no target.
- Keys: 1-7 pick a card, Enter/Space play it, E end turn, P pause, R restart after the duel ends.
- Thirteen original cards in four elements — Flame, Leaf, Tide, Stone — each told apart by colour and shape as well as text.
